# Introduction

Following on from the two previous chapters, we can begin solving partial differential equations of heat-equation type (parabolic equations) by semi-discretizing the equation in space with a second-order centered finite difference method, while keeping time as a continuous variable. The resulting system is a large system of ordinary differential equations, which therefore falls within the framework of the previous chapters.

The [notebook](./edptoedo.ipynb) shows the solution of the heat equation with the first-order explicit Euler method.
