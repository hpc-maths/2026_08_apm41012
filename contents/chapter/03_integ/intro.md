# Introduction

Chapter 3 of the course introduces numerical integration, also called quadrature. Several techniques are used here: Newton-Cotes, in the direct continuation of our work on polynomial interpolation in Chapter 2; Clenshaw-Curtis, which also relies on these notions of interpolation at Chebyshev points; and finally Gauss-Legendre integration.

The first key question that will occupy us in what follows is whether it is better to consider a single interval and increase the order of the quadrature, or to fix the order of the quadrature and increase the number of sub-intervals.
