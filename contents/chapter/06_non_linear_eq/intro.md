# Introduction

Solving nonlinear equations and systems of nonlinear equations is so pervasive in applications that it is up to every numerical analyst to know what is at stake when solving this type of problem. Although the foundations have been known since the 17th century, we had to wait for the work of Simpson in 1740 to reach the form of what we should call the Newton-Raphson-Simpson method!

[This notebook](./examples.ipynb) illustrates the behaviour of Newton's method in several configurations in order to show the difficulties that can be encountered when solving nonlinear equations.
