# Going further

In this section, we shall then address the notion of adaptive time stepping based on a local error estimator, which is very useful in practice for stiff problems; this type of approach is present in many libraries. We then deal with [the system of equations of Belousov and Zhabotinsky](./adaptive_rk.ipynb) (oscillating chemical reactions) and show the efficiency of the local error estimator, the impact of the stiffness and the quality of the adaptive time stepping approach on the global error.
