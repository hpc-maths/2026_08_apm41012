# Essential notions

The goal of the following notebooks of this section is to experiment with [the solution of the advection equation](./linear.ipynb) and [the Burgers equation](./burgers.ipynb) in cases where the exact solution is known (in particular the "little hat"), and to show that we do converge to the weak solution for the advection equation with the upwind scheme, and to the entropic weak solution for Burgers with a Godunov-type scheme. In particular, we observe that convergence and the notion of order then have to be revisited in the framework of weak solutions.

The goal here is not to go into the details of the theoretical aspects covered in the lecture notes, but to experiment and to realize that the framework of hyperbolic equations, of which the proposed examples are generic, requires a different framework from the one introduced for the solution of the heat equation and of the Nagumo equation.
